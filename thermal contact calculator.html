Model notes: Contact conductance uses h = 1.25·ks·(m/σ)·(P/Hc)0.95, with harmonic-mean ks, combined roughness σ = √(σ₁²+σ₂²), combined slope m = √(m₁²+m₂²), and Hc taken as the bulk Vickers hardness of the softer of the two materials. Gap conductance uses hgap = kair/Y with the Yovanovich relative mean-plane-separation correlation Y/σ = 1.184·[−ln(3.132·P/Hc)]0.547. Default σ, m, k, and H values are representative for machined/ground surfaces — replace with measured values for a design-grade result.
