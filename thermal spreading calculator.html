Read me — model & assumptions

This calculation is based on the analytical solution by Lee, Van Au, and Moran (1995) for a concentric circular heat source on a circular, isotropic plate cooled uniformly on its far face.

Ψ(ε,τ,Bi) = Σ (2/√π) · J₁(λₙε) · Φₙ / [ λₙ² · J₀(λₙ)² ] Φₙ = [tanh(λₙτ) + λₙ/Bi] / [1 + (λₙ/Bi)·tanh(λₙτ)] λₙ = n-th positive root of J₁, ε = r_source/r_plate, τ = t_plate/r_plate
Note 1 Source and plate shapes are not important — only their areas. Every area is converted to an equivalent circular radius.
Note 2 The source and plate are assumed to be concentric.
